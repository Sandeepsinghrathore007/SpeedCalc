Competitive Exam Speed Rationale

Breaking the "Audio-Cassette" Playback Loop

When taught linearly (17 × 1 = 17, 17 × 2 = 34...), the brain stores tables as an audio song. Under exam pressure, calculating 17 × 8 forces you to hum from 17 × 1 forward, wasting 6-10 critical seconds. Randomized 2D grid lookups force instant direct retrieval from arbitrary coordinates.

Split & Merge Mental Breakdown

Instead of brute-force memorizing 300 isolated numbers, break 2-digit factors into round base anchors:
• Tens / Twenties Split: 17 × 8 = (10 × 8) + (7 × 8) = 80 + 56 = 136.
• Base Shift: 19 × 7 = (20 × 7) - 7 = 140 - 7 = 133.
• Quarter Rule: 25 × 6 = (600 / 4) = 150.
